## X / Threads thread

**1/** 5 AI stories you need to know this week 🧵

**2/** The DOJ launches a Super Intelligence Task Force. The unit will police misuse of advanced AI by foreign governments, criminal groups and fraudsters, and push AI developers to tighten internal controls and risk reporting. AI misuse is now a federal enforcement priority, not just a policy debate.

**3/** OpenAI's mega-round lands below its target. Reports say OpenAI's new round is priced around $1.25 trillion, down from the $1.4–1.5 trillion it reportedly sought, with about $11B in early commitments. Even the biggest names in AI are meeting tougher investors.

**4/** Big companies start rationing AI tools. Apple reportedly cut daily AI spending caps for some engineers, and Goldman Sachs pulled AI coding tools from traders and bankers, citing productivity and risk. The "unlimited AI" era inside companies is giving way to budgets.

**5/** Data centers lose at the ballot box. One tracker counts data center opponents winning 38 of 39 fully counted local measures in this week's elections. Power and land for AI are becoming a local political fight.

**6/** Thinking Machines Lab nears a $5B raise. Mira Murati's startup is reportedly closing a $5B round led by Accel at a $40B pre-money valuation, and shipped its Inkling 2 Small model this week. Investors are still writing huge checks for frontier-lab challengers.

**7/** That's the week. Follow @theaibrief for next week's brief.
